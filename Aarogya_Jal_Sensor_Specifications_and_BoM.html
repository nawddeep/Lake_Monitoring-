5. Modular Upgrade Pathway & Implementation Strategy

Seamless migration roadmap from Quote 1 to Quote 3 without hardware obsolescence or stranded capital
Zero Sunk Capital: Unified Modbus RTU / RS-485 Architecture
Because all three quotes utilize the identical RS-485 Modbus RTU telemetry bus, ESP32 edge firmware, and cloud ingestion schema, upgrading from Quote 1 to Quote 2 or 3 requires zero firmware redesign. Additional sensors are simply daisy-chained onto the existing Modbus hub.
